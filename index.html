01 / ABOUT

Practical engineering, backed by a scientific mindset.

I work in Radio Unit R&D at Ericsson Nikola Tesla, developing and maintaining C++ software components in a Linux-based telecommunications environment. My day-to-day work combines software development with systematic investigation of behaviour across interacting components.

I enjoy the engineering work behind dependable systems: reproducing difficult issues, tracing root causes, writing targeted tests, and collaborating with colleagues across development, integration and verification.

Before moving into software engineering, I spent several years in physics research, including work at the Jožef Stefan Institute and exposure to the CERN ATLAS software ecosystem. That experience shaped the analytical, evidence-led approach I bring to software problems.
